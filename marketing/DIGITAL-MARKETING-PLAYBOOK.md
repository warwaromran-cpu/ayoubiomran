## How to use this document

For every recommendation identify the business goal, service, audience hypothesis, available evidence, channel role, creative, enquiry destination, follow-up owner, budget assumption and success measure. Cite the applicable sources below. Verify changing features and policies before execution. “Verified” means supported by the named public source or local evidence, not independently audited business performance. “Recommendation” and “hypothesis” are ADIBUILD analysis.

This is a practical starting reference, not an exhaustive platform policy inventory. Regional access, account features, current inventory, legal requirements and campaign eligibility must be checked in the intended account. Do not repeat a platform's global audience figures as evidence of Lebanese customer demand. No ADIBUILD audience demographics, acquisition costs, revenue results or channel baseline have been verified.

## 1. ADIBUILD evidence and brand foundation

ADIBUILD serves two broad business lines: its own residential development marketing and external bespoke woodwork/construction work. Use only current, approved company sources for project facts, prices, availability, construction status, location, or specifications. Historical brochures and earlier posts can be stale; verify project, block, revision, and area definition before use.

The visual family is burgundy and warm ivory/cream, restrained rose rules, serif display type, clean supporting type, generous spacing, and accurate architectural imagery. Exact official fonts and color values remain unverified. Use approved current logo artwork; never recreate it. Label renderings accurately and protect private project/site information.

Audience demographics, acquisition costs, revenue results, and channel baselines have not been independently verified. Treat audience definitions and service geographies as hypotheses until supported by authorized enquiry data, account insights, or owner confirmation.

## 3. Platform playbooks

Audience descriptions below are intended ADIBUILD use cases, not verified platform demographic profiles. Do not assume every ad format or targeting option is offered in Lebanon. Test audience fit before buying reach. Platform and music rights apply to organic and paid creative; do not assume a trending track is licensed for business advertising.

### Instagram — proposed primary visual portfolio

Audience hypothesis: interior-planning homeowners and apartment prospects who inspect the brand before enquiring. Formats: Reels for demonstrations, carousels for explanations, Stories for timely approved updates, profile/highlights for navigation. Discovery can extend beyond followers through recommendations; eligibility is distinct from guaranteed distribution. Meta's public explanation establishes recommendation guidelines, but current account-specific ranking/Insights eligibility was not verified. [Meta recommendation guidance](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/08/recommendation-guidelines/).

Ads: Meta's lead-generation guide documents instant forms and messaging destinations; availability and objective options need account verification. Geographic/audience controls and automation are subject to current category restrictions; do not promise demographic filters for housing ads. Measure available reach, saves/shares, video retention, profile/contact actions and CRM-qualified enquiries separately. Recommendation: one approved project/detail story plus useful planning content; match the landing destination to the promise. No “algorithm hacks” or guaranteed posting time.

### TikTok — proposed process-led discovery test

Audience hypothesis: people discovering interiors/workshop processes rather than actively requesting a quotation. Formats: original vertical demonstrations, narrated design decisions, concise answers; search-oriented captions and recommendation discovery are opportunities to test, not assured reach. Ads Manager documents lead generation via instant forms or website collection, with messaging routes in some implementations. Targeting tools must be verified for the selected region. [Lead generation documentation](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/lead-generation-objective).

Measure video engagement/retention where exposed, landing visits, qualified leads and consultation rate. Recommendation: short authentic footage with one useful question answered, then a relevant portfolio/brief route. Apply advertising and landing-page rules; housing/employment/credit restrictions have explicit geography conditions—do not generalize US/Canada requirements to every market. [HEC policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/housing-employment-credit-hec-ad-policy). No lead-volume forecast or creative-fatigue timetable adopted from generic advice.

### Facebook — proposed local credibility and paid enquiry partner

Audience hypothesis: local communities, existing contacts and people researching the company Page. Formats: project albums, native video/Reels, practical posts and relevant community participation where permitted. Feed recommendations are governed by eligibility guidance; community activity is not permission to spam groups. Ads share Meta infrastructure but creative and placement results must be inspected separately from Instagram.

Meta's historical official guide documents instant forms and messaging lead routes; verify current setup rather than treating 2023 interface instructions as current. [Lead-generation guide](https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/11/LeadGenerationGuide.pdf). Compare Facebook/Instagram qualified-lead cost and downstream progression, not only combined Meta CPL. Recommendation: test clear project/service copy with one destination and a response owner. Current special-category help redirected to login; resolve housing category and targeting in account before launch. [Policy access checkpoint](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/298000447747885).

### YouTube — proposed durable explanations

Audience hypothesis: buyers researching a project and clients evaluating materials/process. Formats: searchable walkthroughs, explainers and Shorts that introduce deeper videos. YouTube documents recommendations across surfaces, including the Shorts feed; no fixed watch-time threshold guarantees reach. [Recommendations](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387).

Google Ads offers video targeting tools; the permitted combination depends on campaign type. [Video targeting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2454017). Measure thumbnail impression CTR, retention, watch time and qualified website enquiries, distinguishing Shorts and long-form reporting. [Analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9002587). Recommendation: answer a recurring client question with original footage and accurate title/thumbnail; use paid amplification only when audience/creative fit is evidenced. Verify copyright, paid-promotion disclosure and ad policies before use; do not assume current duration limits or clickable-link behavior without checking the format.

### LinkedIn — proposed architect/designer partnerships

Audience hypothesis: architects, designers, contractors and project decision-makers. Formats: project/process posts, document explanations, technical collaboration stories and staff expertise with permission. Organic participation is a relationship-building hypothesis; job-title reach is not proof of buying authority. LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms can prefill professional details and connect to CRM; reporting includes CPL and form-fill measures. [Lead Gen Forms](https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/native-advertising/lead-gen-ads-i).

Paid professional targeting should be checked in Campaign Manager by region, audience size and category. Measure relevant conversations, partner opportunities and qualified project briefs. Recommendation: begin with useful organic proof and partner outreach drafts, not broad paid consumer awareness. Verify ad/community policies, privacy and the current permitted lead fields; do not target protected housing traits or send unsolicited automated messages.

### Pinterest — proposed planning-stage discovery

Audience hypothesis: people saving kitchen/wardrobe/interior ideas before selecting a supplier. Formats: descriptive original image/video Pins leading to relevant portfolio/service pages; organize boards by planning intent. Search/saving is a discovery hypothesis to assess through actual outbound traffic, not saves alone. Pinterest documents Ads Manager, Analytics and conversion measurement. [Measurement](https://business.pinterest.com/analytics-and-measurement/).

Ads exist only in supported markets; verify account-country access and available audience/location/keyword/interest controls before recommending spend. [Availability](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/promoted-pins-overview), [location targeting](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/location-language-device-and-gender-targeting). Measure outbound clicks, engaged site visits and assisted qualified enquiries over a longer planning window. [Advertising guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/advertising-guidelines) govern content and data use. Recommendation: repurpose approved detail photography with useful titles; no borrowed moodboard imagery presented as ADIBUILD work.

### Snapchat — optional market-specific experiment

Audience hypothesis: locally reachable users who respond to short visual stories; actual service-market fit unverified. Formats documented for ads include image/video, Story, Collection, Commercial and AR formats. [Formats](https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/advertising/ad-formats). Organic Stories/public discovery should be checked in the intended account before planning distribution.

Snap documents lead-generation ads, audience targeting and insights. [Lead ads](https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/advertising/industry/lead-gen-campaigns), [targeting](https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/advertising/audience-targeting), [Audience Insights](https://businesshelp.snapchat.com/s/article/access-audience-insights?language=en_US). Measure completion/engagement where available, landing actions, qualified enquiries and cost. Recommendation: defer until local demand and vertical-video production are demonstrated. Current housing-policy details were not verified in this review; verify advertising rules, category, age and data restrictions before any test. Do not fund AR production simply because the format exists.

### WhatsApp Business — proposed enquiry and follow-up destination

Audience: people choosing to start or continue an ADIBUILD conversation. Treat it as service/conversion infrastructure rather than assured organic discovery. Business App and Business Platform have different features and messaging controls. Verify which product and number are authorized. Business policy requires appropriate permission and honoring opt-outs; Platform business-initiated conversations/templates and the customer-service window have specific rules. [Current business policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/). Recheck exact requirements before implementing automation; no bulk-message workaround.

Click-to-message ads can feed a conversation, but a click is not a delivered enquiry or sale. Use an approved profile, useful greeting, scoped quick replies and a human response owner where supported. Measure actual new conversations, response time, qualification, consultations and outcomes; ad targeting belongs to the upstream ad account. No customer-list uploads, proactive sending or API subscription assumed authorized.

## 4. Search, local visibility, email and paid search

SEO recommendation: build accurate service pages and project entries answering client questions. Use meaningful titles/headings, accessible images, internal links and crawlable pages; verify indexing through Search Console. Avoid thin duplicated location pages, fabricated reviews, keyword stuffing and ranking guarantees. [Google SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide). Useful queries are hypotheses until query/demand research confirms them: bespoke wardrobes, kitchen cabinetry, doors, and ALÏA apartment enquiries in the actual service market.

Google Business Profile: first verify eligibility, ownership, actual location/service-area representation and public contact details. Google says local results chiefly depend on relevance, distance and popularity. [Local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). Recommendation: accurate categories/hours/services and approved genuine photos; request honest reviews only from real customers without incentives or selective positive-review gating. Track available profile interactions and resulting qualified leads. No listing has been claimed or edited.

Paid search recommendation: separate woodwork and apartment intent, use matching landing pages and inspect actual search terms. Keyword matching considers meaning, so do not equate exact match with literal-only matching. [Matching](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14997606). Add irrelevant-query negatives based on evidence; validate conversions before automated optimization. Verify geo-presence settings, language, budgets, category rules and regional availability. [Housing policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701755). Measure qualified enquiry cost and consultation outcomes, not click volume. Search is useful for existing intent; it does not manufacture demand or prove market size.

Email recommendation: use a permission-based list, separate apartment and woodwork interests, authenticate the sending domain, provide unsubscribe and maintain suppression. No bought lists or automatic sending. Mailchimp documents audience management and compliance. [Audience practices](https://mailchimp.com/help/requirements-best-practices-audiences/), [compliance](https://mailchimp.com/help/about-compliance-for-email-marketing/). Proposed sequence: requested information, answer a relevant planning question, then offer the agreed next step. Track clicks, replies, qualified opportunities, bounces and unsubscribes; treat opens cautiously because privacy tools affect them. Verify automation/integration rules and applicable requirements before activation.

## 5. Content and creative production

Proposed content pillars: verified project explanations; material/design decisions; authentic making/process; useful enquiry/planning guidance. For each piece record ID, goal, audience, factual source/revision, rights, approved wording, channel, format, CTA, reviewer and status. One asset can support several formats but must be recomposed to fit each brief. Any proposed content calendar is a draft workload plan until reviewed.

Creative recipe: one audience question → one clear visual subject → one supported explanation → one appropriate next step. A Reel can show an approved detail, explain why it matters and invite a brief; an apartment carousel can present approved location/layout information without invented stock or scarcity. Do not use AI to regenerate the façade, logo, floor plan or exact copy. Use editable typography and placed originals. Read the design guide before work.

QA: compare against the exact reference; check spelling/accents/units, all requested changes, protected elements, dimensions, mobile readability, alignment, crop, contrast and export. Distinguish visualizations from completion photos. Check current platform format limits, safe overlays, music rights and ad preview before release. Prepare thumbnails/covers deliberately. Approval records must identify exact revision and channel.

## 6. Lead capture, follow-up and conversion

Create one clear destination per intent: apartment details/viewing discussion versus woodwork brief. Landing page should repeat the ad promise, show approved evidence, explain the next step and provide a working contact path. Ask only what is needed: interest/scope, project location, timing, preferred contact; optional budget discussion only when useful and allowed. No sensitive profiling for housing. Maintenance implements/tests technical changes with staging, deployment permission and rollback.

Lead record: stable ID, timestamp, source/UTM, campaign/creative, service, contact permission, response owner, stage, next action/date and reason lost. Suggested stages: New → Contacted → Qualified → Consultation/viewing → Quotation/offer → Won/Lost. Define what “won” means with CEO and Costing; no invented contract, deposit or sales term. Deduplicate repeat messages; keep personal data out of public reports and URLs.

Draft response: “Thank you for contacting ADIBUILD. Are you enquiring about an apartment or bespoke woodwork? Please share [only the relevant missing details]. We can then help with the next step.” Route current availability/prices to verified commercial sources. Recommend a staffed response target agreed with CEO rather than promising 24/7 replies. Follow up at an agreed time; stop when requested. Never send merely because a draft exists.

## 7. Goals, budgets and experiment design

All budgets below are illustrative monthly media envelopes in USD, not approved spend, local CPM/CPL estimates or quotations. Production, tools, management, tax and landing work are separate and unpriced. If budget cannot support meaningful observations, prefer improving assets/follow-up over fragmenting spend across eight platforms.

| Scenario | Proposed focus | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| No paid media | Website/contact accuracy, Instagram proof, eligible GBP, partner content, permission-based follow-up | Establish baseline and fix conversion gaps |
| $300–600 test envelope | One service, one audience market and one paid channel: search if proven demand, otherwise a small Meta lead/message test | Verify platform minimums and eligibility; do not split tiny budgets into many ad sets |
| $1,000–2,000 | One primary acquisition channel plus one controlled creative/destination experiment | Allocate about 70% to current best evidenced approach, 20% to the challenger, 10% reserve; a planning assumption, not a rule |
| Larger approved budget | Scale tested acquisition and selectively test YouTube/TikTok or B2B LinkedIn; Pinterest/Snap only with fit evidence | Increase only when lead quality, sales capacity and marginal acquisition economics hold |

Awareness goal: original Instagram/video explanations; measure relevant reach and engaged visits, then downstream enquiries. Immediate enquiry goal: high-intent search or tested Meta destination plus staffed response. Partner goal: LinkedIn expertise and approved relationship development. Longer planning journey: SEO, YouTube, Pinterest and permission-based nurture. Choose one primary goal per experiment.

Test card: hypothesis; audience/region; creative/source revision; primary metric; acceptable cost/quality threshold; budget cap; duration; response capacity; approval; stop condition. Agree thresholds before spending from actual commercial economics. Change one main variable at a time when feasible. Small samples are directional, not proof; note seasonality and simultaneous changes. No arbitrary universal benchmark or fixed winner after a handful of clicks.

## 8. Measurement and economics

Report weekly operational issues and monthly funnel outcomes. Separate media spend from total marketing cost. Keep platform definitions, reporting timezone, periods, currencies and attribution windows explicit; platform reports can disagree and must not be added as unique people/conversions.

| Measure | Definition / interpretation |
|---|---|
| Qualified enquiry rate | Qualified unique enquiries / unique enquiries; qualification agreed by CEO |
| Cost per qualified enquiry | Media spend / qualified enquiries; show total-cost version separately |
| Contact rate | Leads actually contacted / valid leads |
| Consultation rate | Consultations or viewings booked / qualified enquiries; also record attendance |
| Win rate | Won opportunities / qualified opportunities in a mature cohort |
| CAC | Defined acquisition cost / new customers; disclose included costs and cohort |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue / media spend; not profit, cash collection or proof of incremental sales |
| Contribution after marketing | Verified contribution from acquired work minus relevant acquisition costs; use Costing's scope |

Allowable qualified-lead cost can be modeled as verified contribution per won job × qualified-to-win probability × approved acquisition allocation. All inputs currently unknown; do not calculate a fictional ADIBUILD target. Long property cycles require cohort follow-up; this month's leads and this month's sales need not match.

Use consistent UTMs without personal data. Distinguish contact-link click, message sent, qualified conversation and sale. A WhatsApp link click is only a micro-conversion. Validate analytics with controlled tests after authorization; exclude test records. Return qualified/won stages to platforms only through approved lawful integrations. Pixel/CAPI installation is Maintenance work, not a substitute for consent or accurate CRM records. Use experiments/holdouts when volume permits to test incremental impact; attribution alone is not causality.

## 9. Operating plan and maintenance

First 30 days: confirm CEO priority/service capacity and public contacts; obtain approved kit and rights; verify account access; gather baseline/lead definitions; prepare approved landing copy and content; test contact delivery; request concrete campaign approval only after creative, budget, audience, destination, measurement and follow-up are reviewable. Then launch only within permission and review qualified outcomes.

Owner/CEO decisions to bundle: service priority/geography/languages; active project source and approved availability wording; publish/send authority; staffed response owner; media ceiling and loss limit; account/analytics access; privacy/tracking implementation route. Never request credentials in public files or commit them.

Marketing owns strategy/content and this playbook. Design verifies visuals/project descriptions; Costing verifies commercial claims; Maintenance owns implementation/reliability/security; GitHub handles verified backups; CEO integrates priorities. Use stable request IDs and actual acknowledgments for handoffs.

Update protocol: before any paid launch or policy-sensitive advice, reopen the relevant official sources and verify account/region controls. At least monthly when actively using a channel, review feature/spec/policy changes and record date, source, change, affected recommendation and retest. Review brand corrections as received and performance after each meaningful campaign. This is a working procedure, not an activated monitoring schedule or background service.
