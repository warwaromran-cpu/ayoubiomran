# ADIBUILD Brand and Design Guide

This is a working reference distilled from the existing ADIBUILD marketing study and owner design corrections. Treat supplied approved assets and the current brief as authoritative. It is not a substitute for a complete signed-off brand book.

## Brand observations
- ADIBUILD presents construction and bespoke woodwork. Use verified current company/project sources for all names, locations, status, areas, prices, availability, and claims.
- The visual family uses burgundy and warm cream/ivory, generous whitespace, a restrained secondary rose accent, refined display typography, clean supporting text, fine rules, and strong architectural imagery.
- Keep burgundy and cream consistent with the supplied current master logo/reference. Do not claim a HEX value or typeface is official unless confirmed from an approved brand asset or font file.
- The correct logo is supplied artwork. Place the original without tracing, retyping, stretching, recoloring, or regenerating it. Never substitute a logo from an older draft without approval.
- Use the exact requested wording, spelling, accents (including ALÏA when specified), punctuation, values, and units.

## Architecture and image integrity
- Use approved original building imagery and preserve façade geometry, floor plans, dimensions, and site context. A rendering is not a completion photograph; label it accurately where needed.
- Do not generate a replacement building, invented city background, plan, logo, or project fact. Do not crop away features needed to identify a project.
- Separate source photography, rendering, reference imagery, and old drafts. Confirm rights before using identifiable clients, staff, or private sites.
- Do not copy historical apartment ranges, availability, or dimensions from old posts. Confirm the exact project/block/revision and the meaning of each area from an approved source.

## Layout and typography
- Establish one clear reading order, one focal subject, consistent margins/grid, deliberate spacing, and aligned baselines.
- Use display type sparingly; keep supporting text readable at phone size. Avoid cramped tracking and competing decorative styles.
- Use a supplied licensed font when exact typography is requested. Do not identify a typeface from a visual resemblance alone or silently substitute a lookalike.
- Use color with adequate contrast. Fine rose rules may be decorative, not the only carrier of essential information.
- Match the exact latest reference when asked to make a scoped edit. Preserve all unrelated content and dimensions.

## Production and review
For each asset, record the goal, channel, size, source revision, approved text, requested changes, protected elements, and reviewer. Use editable text and placed original artwork where possible. Compare the export with the source, proof every word/number/unit, inspect at full size and phone size, and verify aspect ratio, crop, contrast, alignment, and text bounds. Deliver an editable source where available and report only checks actually performed. Keep work Draft — Awaiting review until the owner authorizes publication.
