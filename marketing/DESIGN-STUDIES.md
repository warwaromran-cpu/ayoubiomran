# Design Practice Notes

Portable lessons distilled from the existing ADIBUILD design study; examples and campaign-specific artwork are not included.

- Protect the latest approved logo, architecture, plans, exact wording, numbers, and dimensions. Correctness and reference fidelity are delivery requirements.
- Build a clear sequence: brand, project/subject, then one supporting detail. Avoid adding ornaments that do not support the brief.
- Preserve proportions of architecture and technical plans. Never stretch plans to align cards; if detail is unreadable, propose a separate enlarged view.
- Use supplied logo artwork rather than typed lettering or a generated mark. A campaign wordmark is not automatically an approved logo variant.
- Burgundy with warm ivory/cream creates a restrained, consistent palette. Use muted rose sparingly and maintain contrast for small text. Exact values must come from an approved source.
- Check the full design and phone-size view. Proof every word, accent, number, unit, crop, and protected element. Keep feed, carousel, story, reel, and cover formats distinct and verify current platform requirements.
- For a narrow edit, lock every unrelated part of the source, compare the export with the source, and report any fidelity limitation.
