# ADIBUILD Workshop Profile

Status: Reusable business profile compiled from owner-confirmed workspace standards. Verify mutable prices and operational details before using them.

## Business and role structure

ADIBUILD works in construction and bespoke woodwork. The CEO is a separate coordinating role. Design, costing and invoicing, GitHub, software maintenance, and marketing are separate specialists. Specialists report consequential findings to the CEO; the owner retains final approval.

## Drawing and production preferences

- Use millimetres by default; verify units in every incoming source.
- Show a clear overall view, plans, exterior/internal elevations, and relevant sections. Label sides, viewing directions, parts, and dimensions consistently.
- Explain dimensions and construction in plain language. Distinguish overall size, clear space, panel thickness, and fitting allowances.
- Cross-check plans, elevations, sections, cutting lists, and schedules. Record source pages/revisions and mark facts Verified, Proposed, Assumed, or Unresolved.
- Do not infer manufacturing dimensions from photographs or visual scale without verified scale and reproduction size.
- A photorealistic Higgsfield render of each described joinery item was requested as a presentation default. Keep it consistent with the design and label it illustrative. If that tool is unavailable, report the limitation; do not silently substitute a different renderer.
- Manufacturing release requires closed critical issues and explicit owner/designated-person authorization.

## Costing and commercial workflow

- Use the authorized live ADIBUILD price library first. This dated reference snapshot is a fallback for review only; never describe it as a current live check.
- When a new/revised price is provided and authorized, update the matching library entry, preserve dimensions/thickness/currency/unit, avoid duplicates, and verify the saved value. Keep project-only overrides in that project.
- Separate wood materials, hardware, in-house labour, outsourced work, finishing/consumables, electricity, delivery, and overhead as applicable. Identify missing costs; never enter zero for unknown values or double-count included labour/services.
- Use finished panel dimensions for the net cut list, then state edging, joints, machining, waste, and purchasing allowances separately. Do not call a layout optimized unless nesting was calculated with stock, grain, kerf, and trim constraints.
- An optional owner-confirmed selling method is 2 x total project cost before tax. That is 100% markup / 50% margin before tax, not a 100% margin. Use only when the owner requests or approves it; show scope and exclusions.
- Wage-only reference: USD 600 / (26 x 8) = USD 2.884615 per person-hour, based on the saved 2026-10-01 snapshot. This excludes employer costs, productive-utilization loss, overhead, and profit; revalidate before use.
- Workshop electricity reference: approximately USD 400/month as of 2026-10-02. Allocate by a justified job usage basis; never charge the full monthly bill to each job. The allocation driver/capacity is not established here.
